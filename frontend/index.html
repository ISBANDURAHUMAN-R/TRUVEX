TruVex Verification Methodology

Strict Anti-Hallucination Guarantee: TruVex AI never invents links, quotes, articles, or dates. Every single external evidence citation is retrieved directly from live real-time web searches.
1. Multi-Source Diversity: Rather than relying on a single blog or reposted text, our search engine queries across government (.gov), academic (.edu), international wire agencies (Reuters, AP, AFP), and certified fact-checkers (Snopes, PolitiFact, FactCheck.org).
2. Unverified Policy: When reliable evidence is unavailable, we explicitly issue an UNVERIFIED verdict. We never force a FALSE or TRUE decision without corroboration.
3. Old News Recirculation: Viral posts frequently recycle years-old footage as current breaking news. Our timeline checker cross-compares event timestamps to flag archival manipulation.
